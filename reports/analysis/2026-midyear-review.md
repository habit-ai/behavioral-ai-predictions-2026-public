## Methodology and sources

The snapshot uses locked prediction definitions and forecasts, canonical outcomes, and completed review records through 2026-08-15. Later evidence belongs in live monitoring or a later report.

Official Brier scores use only canonical YES and NO outcomes. Each score is the mean squared difference between a locked probability and the binary outcome. VOID, AMBIGUOUS, and TOO EARLY records are excluded.

Each open outlook starts from the equal-weighted median YES probability across all locked forecasters. The analyst probabilities cover YES, NO, and an unscored final state, and sum to 100. The signed delta compares analyst YES with the locked median.

Structured validation checks record coverage, probability arithmetic, band derivation, source linkage, metadata, canonical-state separation, and attestation binding. Source meaning and independence remain reviewer judgments.
